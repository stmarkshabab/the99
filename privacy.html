🐑 The 99 — Privacy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The 99 is a private follow-up tool used by the servants of the St. Mark youth ministry to keep in touch with the youth they care for. It is not a public service, it shows no ads, and it never sells or shares anyone's information.

What the app uses

  • Your Google account email and name — only to check that you are a registered servant on the ministry's list, and to show your name in the app.
  • Youth and follow-up records — names, class, contact details, birthdays and follow-up notes that the ministry already keeps in its own Google Sheet.
  • Notification token — if you turn on reminders, your device gets a code that lets the app send you a reminder. Turning reminders off or signing out removes it.

Where it is kept

Everything stays in the ministry's own Google Sheet, run by the ministry's Google account. The app itself stores only a sign-in key and a short-lived copy of the lists on your device so pages open quickly.

Who can see it

Only servants on the ministry's list can sign in. Each servant sees the youth assigned to them; class leaders and ministry leaders see their class or the whole ministry. Nothing is shared with anyone outside the ministry.

Google services

The app uses Google Sign-In, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script and Firebase Cloud Messaging (for reminders). Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

Ask a ministry leader, or write to stmarkshabab@gmail.com, and your details will be corrected or removed from the sheet.
